Understand Noun Classifications

Learn to effortlessly distinguish between tricky noun forms tested in high-stakes job recruitment exams.

Proper Noun

Refers to specific names of unique people, places, or events.

Ex: Dhaka, Bangladesh, June, Sunday, Kabir

Common Noun

Refers to general items, positions, or living beings of the same class.

Ex: Boy, Pirate, River, Infant, Harbour, Doctor

Collective Noun

Denotes a collection or group of individuals considered as one whole.

Ex: Jury, Team, Flock, Committee, Archipelago

Material Noun

Refers to substances or raw materials from which items are manufactured.

Ex: Gold, Wood, Salt, Milk, Paper, Iron, Water

Abstract Noun

Names an idea, quality, emotion, or state that cannot be seen or touched.

Ex: Bravery, Honesty, Childhood, Height, Length
